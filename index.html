Psalms in Verse sets each psalm as a congregational hymn, one stanza for each verse, kept as close to the Hebrew as the meter allows. Teaching draws on Allen P. Ross, A Commentary on the Psalms (Kregel), Samuel Terrien, The Psalms: Strophic Structure and Theological Commentary (Eerdmans); and David M. Howard Jr. and Andrew J. Schmutzer, eds., Reading the Psalms Theologically (Lexham). Write to contact@psalmsinverse.com · The lyric videos are also on YouTube at youtube.com/@psalmsinverse.
